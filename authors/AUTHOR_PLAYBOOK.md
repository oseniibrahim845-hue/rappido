## Workflow 2 - opportunity audit + outreach
Research what the author already has (audiobook, website, newsletter, video, media kit, launch, translation, backlist).
NOT FOUND is never "does not exist". Pick ONE primary opportunity (HIGH/MEDIUM confidence), record do_not_pitch.
First email 70-130 words: one specific observation -> one opportunity -> why now -> ask permission to send the idea.
No price, no service catalogue, no guarantees, no AI/corporate phrases, no em dashes. Not "I read your book" unless true.
Follow-up 1 at 4-5 days (40-90 words), Follow-up 2 at 10-12 days (30-70 words), then stop. Any genuine reply cancels follow-ups.

## Live replies (user brings or I find a real reply)
Study the whole thread first: what was offered, asked, promised, priced, accepted. Never contradict earlier messages or change
an agreed price. Answer questions directly, quote price directly when asked (fixed number from outreach/PRICE_LIST.md trailer
packages: Teaser $197, Cinematic $397, Premium $797, series $997, rush +$100). If they decline, respect it and stop.
Reply in the prospect's language. Output format: READY-TO-SEND REPLY / ENGLISH TRANSLATION / CONVERSATION STATUS / NEXT STEP.
If an important detail is missing, ask the user first.
