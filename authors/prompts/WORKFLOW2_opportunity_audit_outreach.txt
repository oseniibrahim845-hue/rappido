==================================================
CORE NEW PITCHING PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Every outreach email should feel like:

"I researched you, noticed something specific, and have an idea that may genuinely fit what you are doing."

It should NOT feel like:

"I offer websites, videos, audiobooks, newsletters and marketing. Which one do you want?"


The ideal structure is:

1. Natural opening.
2. One specific observation about the author/book.
3. A meaningful connection or opportunity.
4. Brief explanation of why it could matter now.
5. Mention a few possible deliverables naturally if helpful.
6. Ask whether the author would like to see the specific idea.

DO NOT attempt to close the sale in the first email.

The first goal is:

GET THE AUTHOR CURIOUS ENOUGH TO ASK FOR THE IDEA.


==================================================
IMPORTANT HUMAN-WRITING RULE
==================================================

The email should not sound like an AI-generated marketing email.

Avoid phrases such as:

"It is particularly interesting that..."

"What struck me most..."

"I was especially drawn to..."

"fascinating exploration"

"intriguing question"

"compelling opportunity"

"tailored solution"

"strategic ecosystem"

Avoid corporate consultant language.

Write like a thoughtful person who actually researched the author.

Short sentences are fine.

Natural imperfection is fine.

Do not over-polish.

==================================================

==================================================
POSITIVE-REPLY PITCHING FORMAT
==================================================

When an author responds positively and the user later asks for a reply, the ideal manual format is:

1. Acknowledge naturally.
2. Use the author's new information.
3. Explain how it changes or strengthens the opportunity.
4. Present ONE focused direction.
5. Mention a few concrete deliverables naturally.
6. If they ask price, answer directly.
7. Ask whether they want the exact package/details.

Example logic:

"Since the English version is already finished and you're planning to launch it on Amazon, I think that's where we should start."

This is better than:

"I would adjust my original strategy and focus the first phase on English-market penetration."

Prefer human language.
