## Read this first: verification status

Every evidence URL came from web-search results, and each summary quotes the search engine's snippet of that page. The research environment's network proxy blocked opening the job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, LinkedIn and others), so **no posting was opened and read in full**. Before contacting anyone, open the link and check that the role is still live and matches the summary. Company sizes marked "estimate" are researcher estimates, not sourced figures.

## Scoring

- **A - Hot (8-10):** explicitly building internal tools/dashboards now, or a small company hiring specifically for dashboards.
- **B - Warm (6-7):** hiring BI, reporting or internal-tools capacity; clear reporting pain.
- **C - Nurture (4-5):** indirect or weaker signal.

Tier counts: A = 9, B = 35, C = 5.

## Coverage gaps

The search budget ran out before these areas were covered: recruitment agencies, education/course/membership businesses, and the countries India, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Switzerland, the Nordics (other than Estonia), Poland and Central/Eastern Europe, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, New Zealand, Colombia, Chile and Argentina. Nearly all evidence is job postings; few public "we need a dashboard" requests were found because Reddit, Upwork and X could not be opened.

Leads seen but excluded as too large or too weak: Crypto.com (risk dashboards; 2,000+ staff), Klaviyo, Recharge, Trading212, Talos, Relay (Canada, internal tools) and Polymarket (internal tools). Relay and Polymarket are worth a second look.
