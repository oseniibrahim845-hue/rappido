## PERSONALIZATION IS CRITICAL
Every prospect must receive a genuinely personalized message. Do not use the same opening for every prospect. Research the individual or company first. Find one or more specific facts relevant to their software project, SaaS product, AI application, development workflow, or technology stack. The personalization must mention something real and verifiable (e.g. publicly discusses an AI SaaS product; building an AI-powered app; publicly uses Replit / Lovable / Base44; uses Supabase for backend; publicly requested a developer to finish an MVP; building an AI mobile app; discussing deploying an AI app; migrating from an AI coding platform; a technical problem with their existing app).
- PERSONALIZATION DETAIL: the specific verified fact. Example: "John publicly describes an AI SaaS application he is building with Lovable and Supabase."
- PERSONALIZATION REASON: why that fact makes the prospect relevant. Example: "Because the product is already being developed with Lovable and Supabase, additional development, backend work, integrations, or production deployment could be relevant to the project." Do not claim they definitely need a service unless they explicitly said so.
- PERSONALIZED OPENING: sounds like the sender genuinely researched the prospect. Do not start with "I hope you're doing well", "I came across your profile", "I noticed you are a founder", "Hope you're having a great day". Begin with the specific reason for contacting them. Natural and concise.
